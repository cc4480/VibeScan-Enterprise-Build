### Risks, limitations, and mitigations
- **False positives and noisy scans** — automated scanners can over-report; mitigate with confidence scoring and manual verification workflows.   [HackerOne](https://www.hackerone.com/knowledge-center/website-security-scans-process-and-tips-effective-scanning)  
- **Production impact** — aggressive crawling or form submissions can break apps; provide a “safe mode” (GET-only) and rate limits.   [docs.cloud.google.com](https://docs.cloud.google.com/security-command-center/docs/concepts-web-security-scanner-overview)  
- **Legal/ethical exposure** — scanning without permission can be problematic; require authorization and publish a clear acceptable-use policy.   [HackerOne](https://www.hackerone.com/knowledge-center/website-security-scans-process-and-tips-effective-scanning)
