#### 🟠 HIGH — Missing DMARC Record — No Email Authentication Enforcement
**Category:** Email Security

**Description:** No DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance) record exists for vibe-scan-master.replit.app. Without DMARC, even if SPF and DKIM are configured, there is no policy telling receiving mail servers what to do with messages that fail authentication. Attackers can bypass SPF/DKIM using the "From" header spoofing that DMARC is specifically designed to prevent.

**Evidence:**
```
DNS TXT query: _dmarc.vibe-scan-master.replit.app
Status: NOERROR (domain exists)
No v=DMARC1 record found in 0 TXT record(s)
```
**How to Fix:** Add a TXT record for _dmarc.vibe-scan-master.replit.app:
"v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@vibe-scan-master.replit.app; ruf=mailto:dmarc@vibe-scan-master.replit.app; fo=1"

Start with p=none (monitoring) and progress to p=quarantine then p=reject once you verify legitimate email flows. Use https://dmarcian.com to monitor reports.

**References:** CWE-290 | CVSS 7.5

#### 🟡 MEDIUM — External Resources Missing Subresource Integrity (SRI)
**Category:** Supply Chain Security

**Description:** 1 external script(s)/stylesheet(s) are loaded from third-party CDNs without Subresource Integrity hashes. If any of these CDNs are compromised (which has happened to major CDNs), attackers can inject arbitrary JavaScript that runs on your site for all visitors—stealing credentials, hijacking sessions, or installing skimmers.

**Evidence:**

**How to Fix:** Add integrity and crossorigin to all external resources: `<script src="https://cdn.example.com/lib.js" integrity="sha384-..." crossorigin="anonymous">`. Generate hashes at https://www.srihash.org/ or using `openssl dgst -sha384 -binary FILE | openssl base64 -A`.
